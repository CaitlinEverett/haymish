# Haymish business strategy

## Model

Build a durable independent Mac software business around a mitzvah core and paid professional craft.

### Community

Free, inspectable trust floor:

- read-only inventory
- manual tagging and review
- exact-duplicate evidence
- Keep/protection judgments
- action ledger and undo
- state export
- backup-integrity reporting
- mandatory deletion safeguards

Do not paywall safety, access to a user's own decisions, or recovery.

### Personal

Paid signed/notarized application, likely one-time purchase plus paid major upgrades:

- incremental screenshot workbench
- guided split/merge/refine
- saved routines and review digests
- outlier-first review
- semantic Ask/Find after measured quality
- cache/runtime management
- polished menu-bar and onboarding

Pricing hypothesis: USD 49–99. Validate rather than treating this as a decision.

### Studio

Paid professional seat or support plan:

- shoot workspaces and intent presets
- keyboard contact sheet and side-by-side compare
- portrait/burst quality-and-diversity picks
- asset-family awareness
- ingest and backup manifests
- client/project templates
- XMP/CSV/JSON interoperability
- composite provenance and source-pixel portrait repair
- priority macOS compatibility/support

Pricing hypothesis: USD 199–399 per major version/seat, or USD 15–30 monthly only if ongoing support and compatibility create recurring value.

## Target pilots

Before public launch:

- Caitlin's 28k-asset library: first reviewed canary and screenshot workflow
- 5 personal/family libraries
- 5 working photographers across portrait, event, sports, product, and documentary intent
- 2 professional organizers/archivists

## Metrics

Trust:

- unexplained mutation count: zero
- action/ledger reconciliation
- successful verified undo rate
- deletion-candidate false-positive rate
- protection conflicts caught before action

Value:

- time to first trusted result
- review minutes per 1,000 assets
- corrections per accepted group
- hours saved per professional shoot
- percentage of recurring work skipped through incremental state
- retention of weekly/monthly routines

Business:

- pilot activation and completion
- willingness to place a deposit
- paid conversion by persona
- support time per active installation
- macOS compatibility maintenance cost

## Validation sequence

1. Repair trust floor and make marketing claims honest.
2. Complete a file/tag-only canary and verified undo.
3. Complete a 30-screenshot reviewed pilot.
4. Run concierge pilots; observe decisions instead of asking only for opinions.
5. Offer founding Personal and Studio purchases before building every advanced feature.
6. Decide final packaging from behavior and purchases.

## Licensing

The repository is public and currently unlicensed; that is all-rights-reserved, not an open-source gift. Decide before public onboarding.

Options:

- permissive core (MIT/Apache) plus paid app/support
- copyleft core (MPL/AGPL) plus paid Studio modules
- source-visible paid application
- dual license, with additional legal/operational complexity

Preferred direction to investigate: inspectable/open core for trust-critical adapters, domain, and safety; paid signed Personal/Studio application and support.

## Do not monetize

- user photo data
- ads or model training
- cloud upload by default
- undo or safety gates
- access to exported judgments
- artificial photo-count coercion

## Do not build yet

- pixel editor or full DAM
- cloud photo storage
- social network
- client billing
- cross-platform support
- automatic aesthetic deletion
- broad generative composites before source-derived blink repair is proven
